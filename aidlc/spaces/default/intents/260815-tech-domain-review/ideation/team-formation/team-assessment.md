## Composition: solo

kdiab is maintained by **one person** (Q3=A) working in **occasional focused bursts** (Q1=C). There is no
team to form, no roles to staff, no onboarding. This stage therefore records the operating reality rather
than proposing a team.

| Aspect | Reality |
|---|---|
| People | 1 (the maintainer) — designer, developer, operator, reviewer |
| Cadence | Occasional bursts; no fixed sprint rhythm |
| Delivery model | Solo + **AI-DLC agent personas** as the "virtual team" for this review |
| Decision-making | Single decision-maker; no consensus overhead |
| Key risk | **Bus factor = 1** — everything depends on one person; documentation & evidence-linking (Q8) partly mitigate |

## Implication for the review

The review's recommendations must respect a **one-person operating model**: independently-shippable,
burst-sized items; low operational overhead; nothing that assumes a team to maintain it. This directly
reinforces the architecture-boundary question (RK4 — 9 services for one person).
