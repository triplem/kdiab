= T1D Clinical Glossary
:toc: left
:icons: font

Definitions of clinical and platform-specific terms used throughout the kdiab codebase.

== Glucose Monitoring

=== CGM — Continuous Glucose Monitor
A sensor worn on the body that measures interstitial glucose every 1–5 minutes and transmits readings wirelessly. In kdiab, CGM readings are stored as measures of type `CGM` with a `value` (mg/dL) and an optional `trend` arrow.

=== BGM — Blood Glucose Monitor
A traditional fingerstick meter that produces a single glucose reading per test. More accurate than CGM but less frequent. Stored in kdiab as measures of type `BGM`.

=== SGV — Sensor Glucose Value
Nightscout/CGM-specific term for the glucose value reported by a CGM sensor. In kdiab the canonical field name is `value`, not `sgv`.

=== Trend
Direction and rate of glucose change reported by a CGM (e.g. rising, falling, flat). Encoded as a string arrow or descriptor (`DoubleUp`, `SingleUp`, `Flat`, `SingleDown`, `DoubleDown`).

=== mg/dL — Milligrams per Decilitre
US/conventional glucose unit. Normal fasting range: 70–100 mg/dL. T1D target in-range: 70–180 mg/dL.

=== mmol/L — Millimoles per Litre
European/international glucose unit. Conversion: `mmol/L = mg/dL ÷ 18`.

== Analytics

=== TIR — Time In Range
The percentage of CGM readings within the target glucose range (70–180 mg/dL). Clinical guidelines target ≥70% TIR for most T1D patients. kdiab computes four zones:

[cols="1,1,1"]
|===
| Zone | Range | Field

| Below
| < 70 mg/dL
| `belowCount`

| Target (in range)
| 70–180 mg/dL
| `inRangeCount`

| Above
| 180–250 mg/dL
| `aboveCount`

| High
| > 250 mg/dL
| `highCount`
|===

=== HbA1c — Glycated Haemoglobin
A blood test reflecting average glucose over the preceding ~3 months. Expressed as a percentage (NGSP/DCCT) or mmol/mol (IFCC). kdiab estimates HbA1c from CGM data using the DCCT formula:

[source]
----
HbA1c (%) = (mean_glucose_mg_dL + 46.7) / 28.7
----

=== DCCT — Diabetes Control and Complications Trial
A landmark US clinical trial (1983–1993) that established the link between glucose control and diabetic complications. The DCCT formula for estimated HbA1c is widely used in CGM analytics.

=== AGP — Ambulatory Glucose Profile
A standardised glucose report that shows CGM data as percentile bands over a 24-hour period. kdiab computes five percentile bands per hour bucket (p10, p25, median, p75, p90) and renders them as overlapping area charts.

=== Percentile bands (AGP)

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Band | Meaning

| p10–p90 (outer)
| The outer 80% of readings — the range excluding the highest and lowest 10%

| p25–p75 (inner)
| The interquartile range — the middle 50% of readings

| Median (p50)
| The middle value; half of readings are above, half below
|===

== Insulin Therapy

=== Bolus
An insulin dose given to cover a meal or to correct a high glucose. In kdiab, stored as a treatment of type `BOLUS` with `insulinUnits`.

=== Basal Rate
A continuous low-dose insulin delivery programmed into an insulin pump to cover background glucose production when not eating. Expressed in units/hour.

=== Basal Profile
A time-segmented schedule of basal rates covering 24 hours. A patient may have multiple profiles (e.g. weekday vs. weekend). In kdiab, profiles have a state machine: `DRAFT → ACTIVE → ARCHIVED`.

=== Carb Ratio (CR) / ICR — Insulin-to-Carb Ratio
The amount of carbohydrate (in grams) covered by one unit of insulin. Used to calculate meal bolus doses.

=== ISF — Insulin Sensitivity Factor (Correction Factor)
How much one unit of insulin lowers blood glucose (e.g. 1 unit drops glucose by 50 mg/dL). Used to calculate correction bolus doses.

=== Correction Bolus
An insulin dose given to bring an elevated glucose back into range, calculated using the ISF and the current glucose value.

== Platform Terms

=== BFF — Backend for Frontend
An aggregation service that sits between the frontend and multiple upstream APIs. kdiab-analyze is the BFF: it fetches data from measures, profiles, and treatments on behalf of the analytics frontend.

=== JWT — JSON Web Token
A signed token issued by Keycloak after login. Carries claims including `sub` (user ID), `roles`, `allowed_patients`, `glucose_unit`, and `weight_unit`. Forwarded unchanged from the frontend through the BFF to all upstream services.

=== PKCE — Proof Key for Code Exchange
An OAuth 2.0 extension (RFC 7636) that prevents authorisation code interception attacks. Required for public clients (SPAs). kdiab uses PKCE S256 for all four frontend Keycloak clients.

=== Seed Data
30 days of synthetic CGM readings, treatments, and profiles generated for `sarah` and `mike` by `config/postgres/02-seed-data.sql`. Uses a sine-wave glucose curve to produce realistic-looking AGP output.
