Welcome to the kdiab-carbs documentation hub.
`kdiab-carbs` manages a personal food and carbohydrate database used by the kdiab platform
to support carbohydrate tracking and dose calculation.

== Key Facts

* Patients can create, update, archive, and hard-delete their own food entries.
* Doctors have read-only access to entries of their assigned patients.
* Admins have full access to all entries.
* `carbsForPortion = portionGrams × carbsPer100g / 100` — computed on read, not stored.
* Soft delete via `POST /users/{userId}/foods/{foodId}/archive`; hard delete via `DELETE`.
